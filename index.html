How SkillBridge Works

No subscriptions, no cash transactions. An honest bartering system designed specifically for campus learners.

01

Post Your Skillset

List skills you are confident teaching (coding, graphic design, calculus, music) and skills you are eager to master.

02

Find a Skill Match

Browse the verified campus directory. Send a targeted learning request with your preferred exchange hours and barter offer.

03

Learn & Exchange

Once accepted, use the internal chat to coordinate live sessions. Rate each other after learning to build your campus reputation.
